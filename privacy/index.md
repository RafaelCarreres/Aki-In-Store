# Aki In Store — Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: September 2026*

Aki In Store is a shopping list app organized by store. It is built to keep
your data yours: we do not collect, store or have access to any of it.

## What the app stores

- **Your stores and products.** They are saved on your device. If you are
  signed in to iCloud, they are also synced to your own **private iCloud
  database**, so they appear on your other Apple devices. That database
  belongs to your iCloud account: only you can read it. We, the developer,
  cannot see, read or retrieve its contents. iCloud storage is provided by
  Apple and is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **App preferences** — the last store you had open, the direction of the
  list and whether the store column was showing. They are kept only on your
  device and are never sent anywhere.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your device only.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, sign-in or registration.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDKs or services.
- No servers of our own: nothing is ever sent to us.
- No selling or sharing of data, because we never have any.

## Notifications

The app never shows you notifications and never asks for permission to send
them. It uses silent background updates from iCloud only to keep your lists
current across your own devices.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a store in the app deletes it and its products, on this device and in
your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. You can
also manage the app's iCloud data from your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Personal information

Aki In Store does not collect any personal information. There is nothing to
fill in and nothing about you ever leaves your device, other than your own
lists syncing to your private iCloud.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a
new effective date.
