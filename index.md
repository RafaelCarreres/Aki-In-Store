# Aki In Store — Support

Aki In Store keeps a separate shopping list for each store you visit. This
page covers how to use it and what to do if something doesn't work as
expected.

## Getting started

1. Tap **+** in the left column and name your first store.
2. Type a product in the field at the bottom and press return.
3. Tap a product when you've bought it. It moves to **Purchased**. Tap it
   again to bring it back.

## Stores

- **Show or hide the stores:** tap the store name at the top, or swipe on the
  left edge of the screen.
- **Switch store:** tap it in the left column, or swipe the list sideways —
  left to go to the next store, right to go back.
- **Rename, reorder or delete a store:** press and hold it. You can also drag
  a store to a new position.

Deleting a store also deletes all of its products, and it can't be undone.

## Products

- **Delete a product:** swipe it to the left.
- **Move a product to another store:** press and hold it, then choose the store.
- **Clear bought products:** tap **Clear** next to *Purchased*.
- **Change the list direction:** the button at the top right switches between
  a list that starts at the top and one that grows up from the bottom, closer
  to your thumb.

## Syncing with iCloud

Your lists sync automatically between your Apple devices through iCloud. If
they don't:

- Check that every device is signed in to iCloud with the **same Apple
  Account**.
- In **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**, make sure Aki In Store is allowed to
  use iCloud.
- Give it a moment: changes usually arrive within seconds, but can take longer
  on a slow connection.

Without iCloud, the app works normally and keeps everything on the device.

## Privacy

Aki In Store collects no data. Your lists stay on your devices and in your
own private iCloud. Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://rafaelcarreres.github.io/Aki-In-Store/privacy/).
